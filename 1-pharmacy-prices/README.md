# Project 1: Pharmacy Prices Across Two Districts

**Question:** does the price of a given medicine differ between pharmacies in two districts (A and B)?

**Data:** 29 pharmacy prices (16 in district A, 13 in district B).

**Methods:** descriptive statistics, histograms and box plots, Shapiro-Wilk test (normality), F test (equal variances), Student's t-test for independent samples (α = 5%).

**Main results**
- Mean price: €133.99 (A) vs €138.02 (B).
- Normality and equal variances were not rejected, so a t-test was applied.
- The difference was not statistically significant (t = -0.915, p = 0.368).

**Conclusion:** no evidence that the district affects the price, so there is no advantage in travelling to a specific district. Prices vary considerably within each district.
